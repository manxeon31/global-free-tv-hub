What you can watch

Atlas is a curated discovery app. The initial collection links to broadcaster viewing pages. A checked page means we could inspect its official viewing destination; it does not certify that a live stream plays in your country or that we have embedding rights. Candidate links are labeled when verification was blocked or unavailable.

Where discovery begins

IPTV-org ↗ publishes community playlists. Its JSON API ↗ describes channels, feeds, streams, logos, and guide mappings. Free-TV/IPTV ↗ maintains another community playlist. These are discovery inputs for review, never automatic playback approval.

Our playback policy

Direct playback requires an HTTPS feed, documented approval, a current review, known territorial eligibility, and no DRM. We do not bypass access controls, geography, tokens, subscriptions, or browser access restrictions. Provider ads and branding must remain intact. A feed that cannot play in a browser falls back to its official viewing page.

Your collection

Saved channels stay in this browser using local storage. There is no Atlas account or cross-device synchronization. Broadcaster websites have their own privacy and cookie policies. This app uses Google Fonts; a production deployment can self-host fonts to remove that request.

Corrections & rights requests

For a broken link or rights concern, open a repository issue ↗ with the channel ID and public details only. Do not post private documents or credentials. Production launch requires a monitored private rights contact and removal process.

Source review: November 1, 2026. This collection does not claim comprehensive worldwide availability. Channel names identify their respective broadcasters; Atlas is not affiliated with them.
