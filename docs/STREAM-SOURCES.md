# Streaming-source review

Reviewed November 2, 2026. All 41 previously external-only station entries were reviewed. Twenty-three official YouTube candidates were found; NTN24 disabled embedding in the in-app test. The remaining 22 were enabled, bringing Atlas from 9 to 31 in-app entries. Nineteen entries retain official-site viewing.

The 22 newly enabled players all reached the provider-ready state on the deployed Atlas origin. Actual video playback was additionally confirmed for Sky News and Thai PBS. These tests do not establish availability in every browser or country. Existing player and feed tests also passed.

No unofficial IPTV restream, paid-service URL, copied token, DRM workaround, or geo bypass was enabled. Provider controls and ads remain intact. ABC News Australia’s supplied embedding tool is limited to non-commercial use; the current Atlas prototype is unmonetized.

## Maintenance and limits

The source check used broadcaster-owned channel pages and official viewing destinations. Search-engine video results were treated as discovery only; current Live pages or LIVE cards supplied the selected IDs. Canal Siete’s linear feed was selected separately from its individual programs. BFM2, KBS KOREA, and the KBS K-pop stream were not silently substituted for different stations.

NHK’s YouTube feed focuses on news and may omit parts of the full NHK WORLD television schedule. Thai PBS is a scheduled program stream. NBC and ABC US use daily broadcasts; these and the scheduled Thai PBS entry have short review expiries. Every configured YouTube entry has a Latest official live broadcast link for provider-side changes. Fixed video IDs can end; Atlas does not claim automatic discovery of replacement videos.

FashionTV’s own live page embeds Dailymotion video x3m6nld, but that player returned Forbidden in the test browser. The source was documented rather than presented as working. A matching, working, provider-authorized in-app source is still needed for the remaining external entries.

LCP fallback correction: the former /le-live page returned a missing-page notice. The current LCP homepage directs viewers to the official france.tv LCP/Public Sénat live page; Atlas now uses that destination.
